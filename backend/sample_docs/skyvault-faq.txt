SkyVault Cloud Storage — Frequently Asked Questions (fictional sample document)

This is a fictional product FAQ created for demonstrating the AI Document Assistant.

What plans does SkyVault offer?
SkyVault has three plans. Free includes 5 GB of storage. Plus includes 200 GB for 149 rupees per month. Team includes 2 TB shared storage for up to 10 users for 999 rupees per month.

How long are deleted files kept?
Deleted files stay in the Recycle Bin for 30 days on the Free plan and 90 days on the Plus and Team plans. After that they are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Team plan administrators can additionally enable customer-managed keys.

What is the maximum file size?
The maximum size of a single file is 2 GB on the Free plan and 50 GB on paid plans.

How do I share a file?
Right-click a file and choose Share. You can create a view-only link, an edit link, or invite specific people by email. Links can be protected with a password and an expiry date on paid plans.

Which devices are supported?
SkyVault has apps for Windows 10 and later, macOS 12 and later, Android 10 and later, and iOS 16 and later. You can also use the web app in any modern browser.

How do I contact support?
Free plan users can use the community forum. Plus users get email support with a response within 48 hours. Team users get priority chat support during business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST.
