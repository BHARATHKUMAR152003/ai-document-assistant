# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy (fictional sample document)

_Version 2.1 · This is a fictional document created for demonstrating the AI Document Assistant._

## 1. Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for remote work. Interns and
contractors may work remotely only with written approval from their team lead.

## 2. Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to **three days per week**. Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated collaboration days,
when everyone is expected in the office unless they have an approved exception. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local
time; employees must be reachable on chat during core hours.

## 3. Equipment and expenses

Northwind provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of
**₹15,000**, claimable within the first six months of starting remote work. Internet costs are reimbursed up to
₹1,000 per month on submission of a bill.

## 4. Security

Company data must only be accessed through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected.
Printing confidential documents at home is not allowed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within
**24 hours**.

## 5. Requesting an exception

To work remotely on a collaboration day, submit a request in the HR portal at least two working days in advance.
Requests are approved by the team lead. Medical exceptions are handled confidentially by HR.
